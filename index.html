Topic reference: New Science in Everyday Life, Oxford University Press, by Vaishali Gupta and Anuradha Gupta, as identified by the family. All explanations, questions, and graphics here are newly created. No textbook scans, illustrations, OCR, or exercises are reproduced. This is not an Oxford University Press product or endorsement.

Buddy uses local, curated explanations and word matching, not an external AI model. It may ask for clarification or say a question is outside the guide. The app has no analytics, logins, stored transcripts, or question API. The hosting provider still receives ordinary requests for the website’s files.

This is temporary exam revision, not medical advice or an experiment guide. For health worries or activities involving heat, chemicals, or sharp tools, children should ask a trusted adult. Follow the textbook and teacher for exam wording.
